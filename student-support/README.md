# Student Support — Solving Common Cybersecurity Learning Barriers

Our goal is to reduce the friction between **wanting to learn security** and **getting meaningful hands-on practice**.

| Common pain point | GHC learning approach | Practical guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "I cannot afford a high-end laptop." | Begin with free fundamentals; use browser-based lab environments where eligible | [Low-cost learning](./low-cost-learning/README.md) |
| "Kali, VMs and networking won't install." | Use preconfigured approved lab environments rather than repeatedly rebuilding a local stack | [Lab access](./lab-access/README.md) |
| "I watch videos but can't explain what I learned." | Follow concepts with goals, guided practice, evidence and review | [Getting started](../getting-started/README.md) |
| "I don't know which certification to take." | Choose based on current fundamentals and target role, not hype | [Certification plan](../certification-plan/README.md) |
| "My GitHub and resume are empty." | Turn original lab notes into a safe portfolio and evidence-based resume | [Career guidance](../guidance/README.md) |
| "I don't know how real security teams work." | Learn the context behind service workflows such as scoping, assessment, findings, remediation and retesting | [Skills to services](../industry-practice/README.md) |

## Access expectations

Foundation materials are public. Academy lessons, browser labs, paid courses and assessments may require enrollment and authentication; current access terms are published on the official sites. Browser-based does not mean offline or zero cost. You still need an internet connection and a capable browser.

## When something is not working

1. Confirm you are using the official Academy or lab link.
2. Check that your login and membership are valid for the resource.
3. Try an updated browser and stable network.
4. Record the exact error, time, browser and lab title; **never share passwords, session cookies or tokens**.
5. Use the official support/contact route and include sanitized troubleshooting information.
