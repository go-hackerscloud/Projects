# Instructor Assessment Rubric — 100 Points

This rubric is for **educational feedback**, not industry certification, recruitment screening, or professional authorization.

| Dimension | Points | Strong evidence |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Objective, prerequisites and permission boundaries | 15 | Accurate goal, owned/synthetic/authorized scope, clear exclusions |
| Conceptual understanding | 15 | Student explains why observations and controls matter |
| Reproducible process and evidence | 25 | Clear steps, honest outcomes, traceable sanitized evidence |
| Findings, uncertainty and remediation | 20 | Evidence-backed reasoning, no invented impact, actionable proposals |
| Validation, cleanup and reflection | 10 | Retest logic, limitations, safe cleanup and learning insight |
| Portfolio communication | 15 | Readable README/report, professional writing, accurate claims |
| **Total** | **100** | |

## Suggested interpretation (not a certification threshold)

| Score | Instructor guidance |
| --- | --- |
| 85–100 | Strong educational submission, minor or no revisions |
| 70–84 | Meets many objectives, targeted improvements advised |
| 50–69 | Partial understanding, resubmit selected sections |
| Below 50 | Significant coaching and another practice attempt recommended |

**Safety gate:** Regardless of points, publication is **not approved** if a student exposes secrets, real customer data, unauthorized target information, or falsely claims a real client engagement. Correct those issues before sharing.

## Fair assessment principles

- Score the work actually submitted; do not infer activities from screenshots alone.
- Accept valid low-spec-device or offline alternatives where the objectives are met.
- Provide a reasonable chance to revise, and adapt format needs when possible.
- Do not award points for unsafe actions, unapproved scanning, unauthorized access or publication of secrets.
- A purely synthetic project may correctly state that live testing and retesting were not possible.
- Certification study roadmaps should not be treated as official vendor exam criteria.
