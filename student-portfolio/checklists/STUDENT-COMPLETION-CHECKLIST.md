Use this checklist for Foundation, certification practice and Professional Practice Series assignments. Complete it **before** publishing to GitHub or attaching your work to a resume.

## Learning and technical quality

- [ ] I can describe the objective without reading the instructions.
- [ ] I understand the required prerequisites.
- [ ] I used an owned, synthetic or explicitly authorized environment.
- [ ] My methods and steps are reproducible where appropriate.
- [ ] I recorded **actual results**, including unexpected results.
- [ ] I can separate observations from assumptions.
- [ ] I wrote a remediation or improvement proposal when relevant.
- [ ] I included a realistic validation or retest plan.
- [ ] I performed cleanup where applicable.
- [ ] I cited references and credited third-party sources.

## Professional presentation

- [ ] A clear README identifies the purpose and learning level.
- [ ] The architecture diagram accurately reflects the environment.
- [ ] Screenshots, tables and code blocks are readable.
- [ ] My report explains business relevance without exaggeration.
- [ ] I can explain the project to an interviewer in 2–3 minutes.
- [ ] I did not claim employment, production experience or certification on the basis of this lab.

## Safety and privacy

- [ ] All targets were approved for my practice.
- [ ] No credentials, cookies, keys or access tokens are included.
- [ ] No client names, internal systems or private customer data are included.
- [ ] No proprietary course solutions or exam dumps are published.
- [ ] Synthetic content is labeled as synthetic.
- [ ] Findings are not marked validated or closed without evidence.
