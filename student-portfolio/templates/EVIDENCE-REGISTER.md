## Rules for useful evidence

1. Give every observation a unique ID and reference it in the related finding.
2. Describe **what the evidence actually demonstrates**. Keep guesses in the interpretation column.
3. Record tool versions or timestamps where relevant and available; do not invent missing metadata.
4. Do not publish session cookies, access keys, passwords, confidential paths, client identities or paid course answers.
5. Clearly label fabricated fixtures as **synthetic**.
6. Mark any evidence you could not verify as **unverified**, not confirmed.
7. Do not change evidence records after a review without documenting the revision.
