# Start Learning with GHC — From Your First Day to Career Readiness

Cybersecurity education should not begin with buying an expensive workstation or debugging a complicated virtual machine.

**GoHackersCloud's approach:** learn the concepts first, use guided browser-accessible labs where your enrollment includes them, document what you learn, and build credible portfolio evidence.

GoHackersCloud reports having trained **10,000+ learners worldwide**. This is an organization-reported milestone, not an independently audited count or a claim of 10,000 certifications or job placements.

## The GHC learning cycle

1. **Understand:** read a Foundation lesson and restate the concept in your own words.
2. **Observe:** follow an Academy example and identify the system, threat, and expected control.
3. **Practice:** launch an authorized sandbox when accessible, or use a small local no-cost exercise.
4. **Explain:** capture sanitized evidence; describe what happened and why it matters.
5. **Assess:** use review questions, a test engine when available, or an instructor's checklist.
6. **Demonstrate:** publish a clear, permission-safe GitHub write-up and explain it in an interview.

## Example: one concept, one workplace skill

**Concept:** least privilege.

**Practice:** in a synthetic lab, compare normal-user versus administrative permissions.

**Evidence:** a short before/after permissions table without secrets.

**Workplace connection:** identity and access assessments ask whether users and services have more permissions than their duties require.

**Portfolio artifact:** a one-page access-control review with a recommendation and retest plan.

GHC Labs are practice environments; they are **not live customer engagements**, and completing a lab does not authorize a learner to assess third-party systems.

## Suggested first seven days

| Day | Work | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Identify your role goal; complete IT basics | Personal study plan |
| 2 | Networking: DNS, IP, TCP, HTTP | One-page diagram |
| 3 | Linux: files, permissions, processes | Command observation notes |
| 4 | Security: threats, controls and scope | Short concept summary |
| 5 | Try a local exercise or eligible GHC lab | Sanitized screenshot / notes |
| 6 | Write findings and remediation | Mini report |
| 7 | Review knowledge gaps and plan next week | Updated learning plan |

There is no fixed completion guarantee. Follow your own pace.
