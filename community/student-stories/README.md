# GHC Student Stories — Learning Journeys and Project Showcases

Every learner's progress matters. This is a **submission-ready** place to showcase real student learning experiences, accessible practice, and portfolio work.

**Publication status:** We have not yet published individually consented and verified student stories in this collection. A template is not a testimonial. No placement, salary increase, certification pass, or course-completion claim should appear without appropriate permission and evidence.

## Why share your story?

Learners can explain how they started, what barriers they faced (expensive laptops, VM setup, unclear learning paths), how they learned fundamentals and practiced securely, and what they created. This helps peers understand the journey without promising the same outcome.

## Suggested story categories

| Category | Example of suitable evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Starting from fundamentals | Self-authored learning plan, sanitized notes or concept diagram |
| Learning with limited hardware | Description of browser-based or offline practice, with accurate access conditions |
| Practical projects | A public original GitHub README and safe evidence |
| Certification preparation | Study roadmap and skills review; **not** a claimed pass without permission and verification |
| Career development | Evidence-based portfolio improvement or an honestly reported professional milestone |
| Community contributions | Reviewed documentation or original project PRs |

## How to submit

1. Read the [publication and consent policy](./PUBLICATION-POLICY.md).
2. Prepare a story using the [submission template](./STORY-SUBMISSION-TEMPLATE.md). Do not include a legal name, email, phone number, employer identity or photographs in a public PR unless specifically authorized for publication.
3. Prepare your project using the [Student Portfolio Toolkit](../../student-portfolio/README.md).
4. Ask maintainers for a **private submission path** through the [official GHC contact page](https://gohackerscloud.com/contact-gohackerscloud) if the story includes identifying details, testimonials or verification evidence. Do not send private consent records through public issues.
5. After review and explicit publication approval, a maintainer may publish a minimal, approved version.

## What will be reviewed?

- Is the work real, accurately described and yours to share?
- Are all scope, outcome, certification and employment statements supported?
- Is the quoted wording the student's own, or has the student approved the edited wording?
- Is the requested public display name and linked profile appropriate?
- Are screenshots, customer information and secrets removed?
- Is consent documented privately and revocable through a published contact route?

**We will not invent stories, use composite personas as testimonials, or imply employment with GHC Cybersecurity Services from completing labs.** The organization-reported 10,000+ learner milestone is not a verified individual-outcomes database.
