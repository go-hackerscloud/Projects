# Student Story Publication & Privacy Policy — Editorial Draft

**Status:** Working publication standard for this GitHub repository. This is not a substitute for a reviewed organizational privacy notice or a complete legal consent mechanism.

## Core principles

1. **Voluntary participation:** learners may use public resources without telling their story or agreeing to marketing.
2. **Specific permission:** permission for a GitHub story is separate from consent for website ads, email campaigns, social posts or reuse of an image. Obtain each separately.
3. **Minimum information:** use an alias or anonymous attribution by default. Avoid unnecessary personal details, student IDs, phone numbers, grades, employer names and precise locations.
4. **Evidence-based outcomes:** distinguish completion of a learning exercise from certification, employment and customer delivery. Do not manufacture testimonial quotes or inflate reach.
5. **Private review:** consent, contact information and supporting private evidence must not be stored in the public Git repository, pull request, public issue or commit history.
6. **Editorial approval:** show the learner the exact proposed publication (text, images and links) and retain a dated private record of approval.
7. **Withdrawal and correction:** provide an official contact route for requests. Promptly remove or correct content under GHC control when appropriate, while explaining that Git history, forks, caches and third-party copies may persist.
8. **Additional safeguards:** do not solicit stories directly from minors for public publication without a separately reviewed guardian-consent and safeguarding process.
9. **No false implication:** being featured does not mean the learner is employed, certified, endorsed or hired by GHC.

## Maintainer approval gate

Before publishing a student story:
- [ ] Contributor owns the story and submitted it voluntarily
- [ ] Permission for the specific public text, attribution and links recorded privately
- [ ] Any photo or screenshot has separate authorization
- [ ] Certification or career outcome phrasing reviewed against evidence
- [ ] All private and proprietary data redacted
- [ ] No sponsorship, incentive or material relationship omitted where relevant
- [ ] Learner has an official correction/withdrawal contact path
- [ ] Public Markdown diff reviewed by a second maintainer

## Correction or withdrawal request

Use [GoHackersCloud's official contact page](https://gohackerscloud.com/contact-gohackerscloud). Include the published story URL and the requested correction/removal without posting additional private details publicly.

**GitHub caveat:** deleting or editing a repository file does not reliably erase historical commits, forks, index caches or screenshots; never upload sensitive source material at all.
