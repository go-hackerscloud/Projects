# Community Showcase — Invitation Copy

Use these drafts when inviting submissions. They are **proposed communications**, not messages already sent or evidence of participation.

## Community message

**Show what you've learned with GoHackersCloud!**

Have you built a Linux, networking, ethical hacking, cloud security or cybersecurity portfolio project? We're preparing the **GHC Community Showcase** to highlight original learner projects and help others learn from real, documented experience.

You don't need an expensive laptop, a job offer or a certification to submit. A clear project with safe evidence, a learning objective and honest reflection matters more than flashy screenshots.

Explore the submission guide: https://github.com/go-hackerscloud/learn-with-ghc/tree/main/community/showcase

Please share only material you own and are comfortable publishing. Never upload credentials, private customer data, paid course answers or personal consent records to GitHub. Selected contributions are subject to review and approval.

**Participation is voluntary; publication is not guaranteed.**

## Email invitation

**Subject:** Share your cybersecurity project with GHC Community Showcase

Hello,

We're inviting learners and community members to propose original projects for the GHC Community Showcase. Examples include Linux security fundamentals, local HTTP investigations, offline cloud IAM reviews, and professional-style reports from synthetic exercises.

A strong submission explains what you learned, shows sanitized evidence, and clearly identifies what was and was not tested.

Read the guidelines at https://github.com/go-hackerscloud/learn-with-ghc/tree/main/community/showcase and submit a non-sensitive public project proposal through GitHub Issues.

If you would prefer to share a personal story or identifiable achievement, please contact us privately first so we can review publication permission separately.

Regards,  
GoHackersCloud Community Team
