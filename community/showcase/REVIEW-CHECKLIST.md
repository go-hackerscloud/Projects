# Community Showcase Review Checklist

The showcase must be useful, safe, fair and credible. Perform review before publishing an individual entry.

## Public project quality

- [ ] The submission is original or explicitly licensed for the contributor's use.
- [ ] The stated learning objective is specific and achievable.
- [ ] The environment is local, synthetic or explicitly authorized.
- [ ] Steps and expected observations are coherent and accurately described.
- [ ] Claims distinguish observations from interpretations and uncertainty.
- [ ] A sanitized public artifact or report is accessible.
- [ ] A less-powerful-device alternative or hardware requirements are described.
- [ ] Any source code or external references are properly credited.

## Privacy and ethical review

- [ ] No secrets, session cookies, student identifiers, customer artifacts or confidential data.
- [ ] No proprietary course solutions, unauthorized scanning or leaked exam questions.
- [ ] A public attribution choice has been approved by the contributor.
- [ ] The public project URL and summary were checked before publication.
- [ ] Any identifiable quotation, image, certificate or personal success story follows the separate [publication policy](../student-stories/PUBLICATION-POLICY.md).

## Editorial decision

| Outcome | When appropriate |
| --- | --- |
| Ready to feature | All required checks pass |
| Request revisions | Minor issues with scope, reproducibility or documentation |
| Keep private / decline | Missing rights, unsafe data, unverified sensitive claims or lack of publication permission |

The checklist must be applied consistently. A learner cannot buy placement in the showcase, and inclusion is not a promise of jobs, certification or endorsement.

## Publication record (keep private as needed)

Track the public project URL, agreed public attribution, review status, reviewer and date. **Do not commit private identity or consent records to the public repository.**
